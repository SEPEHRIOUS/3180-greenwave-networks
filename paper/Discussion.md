The research reviewed in this project shows that AI-native 6G networks have the potential to greatly improve future wireless communication through intelligent automation, better resource management, and improved network performance. At the same time, many studies highlight challenges related to privacy, security, and consumer trust because these networks rely on collecting and processing large amounts of data. While technologies such as federated learning and AI-based security can help address these concerns, further research is still needed to build secure and trustworthy AI-native 6G networks.
